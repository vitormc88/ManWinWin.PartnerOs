# Prototype case-study selection — 8 October 2026

Five English-language public articles were read in full and matched by numeric customer ID and documented project scope. This is an editorial source review, not independent verification, customer approval, ROI validation or confirmation of active status. The prototype does not change sectors, lifecycle, source records or production data.

| Customer | Scope | Useful for | Evidence limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| H.B. Fuller Portugal | Portuguese implementation; UK use mentioned | Pilot rollout, preventive maintenance, materials and mobility | Initial asset/material counts; no quantified savings comparison |
| SANA Hotels | Group project across Portugal, Germany and Angola | Multi-site rollout and cross-department requests | Group totals, not Portuguese-site outcomes; totals lack reporting date |
| ENAPOR | Cabo Verde ports | Legacy-data migration and inter-port standardisation | Qualitative benefits, no before/after KPIs |
| Watsons Philippines | Philippines store network | Distributed retail requests and remote training | Volume/adoption rather than ROI; mobility limitations remain |
| Laboratório Edol | Portuguese laboratory | Paperless requests, calibration and technical records | Time-logging coverage is not productivity uplift; fleet adoption slowdown |

Sources: https://www.manwinwin.com/case-study-h-b-fuller/ ; https://www.manwinwin.com/case-study-sana-hotels-manwinwin/ ; https://www.manwinwin.com/case-study-enapor-manwinwin/ ; https://www.manwinwin.com/case-study-watsons-philippines-manwinwin ; https://www.manwinwin.com/case-study-laboratrio-edol-manwinwin . The latter two were read from the official live HTML when the web reader was unavailable.

The public article remains the content source of truth. Prototype summaries are deliberately short, identify their scope and evidence limits, and carry the source review date. Updates to the article do not automatically validate or refresh the summary. Additional cases require the same review before inclusion. No article is automatically linked using a logo filename or group-brand match.

For production, move curated case-study metadata into HQ-managed storage, define an editorial owner and review cadence, and preserve explicit association scope. Public reading is separate from requesting a customer introduction through the responsible contact. The current prototype-only content is not deployed.
